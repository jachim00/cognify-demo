/ what Bolek is

Munkatárs, aki nem kér péntek délutáni szabadnapot.

Imagine a team of juniors with senior-level skills, working in fifty browser windows at once. No coffee breaks, no sick leave, no "I'll finish it tomorrow". That's Bolek — except without a desk, payroll, or national holidays.

Where a person processes one document per minute, Bolek processes five hundred. You come back Monday to 200 new emails — Bolek wrote 180 draft replies overnight and flagged 20 needing your decision. The monthly report that used to take two days of your life? Ready at 6:00 AM on the first of the month, as a PDF, with conclusions.

No magic. Underneath: solid engineering — FastAPI, Celery, Postgres, Redis wired to Claude 4.7 models (and cheaper ones, where they suffice). For you it looks different — a browser panel showing queue size, completed jobs, what's waiting for your "ok", and exactly what happened overnight while you slept.

/ lehetőségek

What Bolek really does.

Features that move your P&L. Not buzzwords — measurable outcomes.

Smart task queue

Each task has a priority, deadline, and weight. Bolek decides what to run first — urgent and important goes to the strongest AI, bulk to the cheaper one. You see the full queue in the panel and can reorder it with one click.

Parallel execution

Instead of one-by-one — dozens at a time. Bolek scales dynamically with the queue size. More workers during peak hours, fewer at night. You pay only for actual usage.

Real-time result streaming

No need to wait for completion. Bolek shows the result as soon as it's ready — in the panel, on Slack, via email, or webhook to your system. Decide immediately, not an hour later.

Auto-retry on errors

Internet down? Provider API returned 500? Bolek retries with exponential backoff (1s → 2s → 4s → 8s). After 5 failed attempts — escalation to a human with full context.

Full audit log

Who, what, when, which model, how much it cost, what the result was. Every Bolek operation is logged immutably. GDPR and AI Act compliance — sorted. External audit? Logs from every single minute.

Webhooks & integrations

Bolek talks to your CRM, ERP, Slack, email, calendar, Trello, Notion, Google Sheets, Airtable. What happens — flows to your system. What your system changes — Bolek learns about it.

Cost control

Każdy task ma swój kosztowy ślad. Możesz ustawić budżet dzienny/miesięczny — Bolek się w nim mieści. Drogie modele tylko gdy potrzebne, tańsze gdy wystarczą. Średnio 60–80% niższy rachunek niż "wszystko na Opusie".

Human-in-the-loop

Krytyczne decyzje (np. odpowiedź klientowi VIP, akceptacja faktury > 10 000 zł) zatrzymują się i czekają na zatwierdzenie. Twój zespół klika "ok" lub "popraw" — Bolek się uczy z każdej korekty.

/ gyakori kérdések

Amit az ügyfelek általában kérdeznek.

Will Bolek replace my employees?

No — though we get the concern. Bolek takes routine: categorizing, classifying, drafting, sorting. Your people start doing what you really hired them for: talking to difficult clients, making decisions AI shouldn't, designing new processes. In practice, teams using Bolek don't cut staff — they stop hiring more as the business grows. That's the difference.

What if Bolek makes a mistake?

Every critical decision (invoice above amount X, reply to VIP client, refund approval) pauses for human review. The mass ones — e.g., classifying an email as "order status" — Bolek does itself, but leaves a trace: "92% confident, source here". If wrong, the operator clicks "fix" and the system learns from every correction. Within two months accuracy rises from 85% to 96%.

Is my data secure?

Bolek can run on-prem (on your servers), in your cloud, or hybrid. In every configuration, data never leaves your infrastructure. Communication with AI models goes through private endpoints (Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, dedicated local LLMs) without content logging on the provider's side. Full audit log, GDPR and AI Act compliance — included in the implementation.

How much does it cost per month?

Implementation is a one-time project cost (depends on scale). Monthly upkeep has two components: AI model cost (you pay for actual usage — €200–1000/month average for a mid-sized company) plus optional retainer for our team (from ~€700/month for 24h responsiveness). Most clients recoup the implementation cost in 3–6 months on team time savings alone.

Where do we start?

With one process. The one your team loses the most time on, that nobody enjoys. Email inbox? Invoices? Ticket categorization? You pick one, we build Bolek for that specific case in 4 weeks, you test for a month whether it does what it should. If yes — we expand. If not — we stop, no long-term commitments.
