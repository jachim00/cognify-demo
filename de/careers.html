Was Sie hier erwartet.

Echtes Produkt, echte Kunden

No POCs that end up in a drawer. Every project ships to production for a paying client. Your work is measured by impact, not closed tickets.

Modernster Stack

Claude 4.7, GPT, Llama, FastAPI, Next.js 15, MCP, pgvector. No struggling with a decade of legacy, but we don't chase every trend either — we pick what works.

Kleines Unternehmen, kurze Wege

Team of up to twenty people. Decisions made the same day, not after three committees. Your idea for a new project can launch on Wednesday.

Remote-Arbeit, eigener Rhythmus

We have an office in Warsaw, but 80% of work is remote. Flexible hours — we care about outcomes, not attendance. 4 weekends per year of in-person team workshops.

dev AI Warschau / Remote B2B / employment contract mid / senior

AI Developer / ML Engineer

Work on the core of our systems — agents, RAG pipelines, Claude integration via MCP. Code you write ships to a client's production within the same week.

Was Sie tun werden

  • ▸Build AI agents on Claude 4.7 and other models (task orchestration, tool use, function calling, MCP).
  • ▸Design retrieval pipelines (RAG): embedding, indexing, re-ranking, evaluation.
  • ▸Write FastAPI backends with tests, monitoring, and production observability.
  • ▸Integrate with clients' CRMs, ERPs, and tools. Mailboxes, calendars, databases.
  • ▸Optimize model costs — per-task routing, caching, batching.

Was wir erwarten

  • ▸2+ years of commercial Python experience. Type hints, mypy, tests — standard.
  • ▸Real projects with LLMs behind you (not just online courses). You know the difference between temperature 0.2 and 0.8 and when to use which.
  • ▸FastAPI or similar async framework. Postgres. Redis. Docker.
  • ▸You can read an API doc — even an incomplete one — and ship a working integration in a day.
  • ▸You speak Polish and English — our clients are Polish, the docs are English.
  • ▸Nice to have: vector DB experience (pgvector, Qdrant), eval frameworks, observability (OTEL).

Was wir bieten

  • ▸Compensation: PLN 14,000–22,000 net B2B (mid) / PLN 22,000–32,000 net B2B (senior). Employment contract possible — proportionally lower.
  • ▸Remote work, flexible hours, 26 days of paid 'leave' even on B2B.
  • ▸Learning budget PLN 4,000/year (courses, books, conferences, model credits).
  • ▸A say in what we do — no corporate red tape, no 5-level approvals.

/ Praktika

Erstes echtes Engagement.

Studying computer science, cognitive science, mathematics, or just finished a bootcamp and want to try working with language models? We have a place for you — not to brew coffee, but to do real work on products that ship to clients.

We run internships three times a year: February–April, June–August (student favorite), October–December. Each internship is 3 months at 20–30h/week (negotiable around your schedule).

Each intern gets a mentor (one of our seniors you can call mid-day), a concrete project (not a list of 'rewrite the README' tasks), and a weekly 1:1 with feedback. After 3 months we usually propose continuation — either full-time or help finding work elsewhere if we're not a fit.

bezahltes Praktikum
3.000 PLN / Monat netto

Standard intern compensation (B2B or contract of mandate). For full-time after the internship — junior range from the Product Specialist role.
